# 8D review rubric

Two reviewers score each drafted 8D from 1 to 5 on each line. The eval passes when the mean is 4 or higher.

| Score | Containment (D3) | Root cause (D4) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Quantities and locations of all suspect parts stated | Cause shown by evidence; hypotheses labelled | Every D2–D7 claim cites a record that supports it |
| 4 | Quantities stated, one location missing | Plausible cause, partly evidenced, labelled | One citation weak but relevant |
| 3 | Generic action, no quantities | Generic cause ("operator error") | Citations present but do not support the claim |
| 2 | Containment missing for some stock | Cause contradicts the records | Fields without citations |
| 1 | No containment | No cause | No citations |
